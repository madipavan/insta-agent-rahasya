Small coastal town in Norway. Peaceful. Boring. Perfect for a murder, honestly.

A fisherman pulls up a net and finds a suitcase. Locked. Heavy. Wrong smell.

Inside isn't clothes. It's documents — passports, names that don't match, one photo of a man who's supposed to be dead since 1998.

The town has twelve people. All twelve say they never saw the suitcase before.

All twelve are lying. You just don't know why yet.
