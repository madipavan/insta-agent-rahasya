Her phone rings at 3 AM. Unknown number. She ignores it.

It rings again. Same number. She answers.

A voice says her full name, her address, and then: "Don't open the door."

She's already at the door. Hand on the knob.

On the other side — nothing. No sound. But the handle is warm, like someone was holding it a second ago.
