American thriller energy but set in Budapest, so the vibes are extra.

A journalist checks into a hotel under a fake name. Gets the key. Opens the door.

Someone is already in the room. Sitting in the dark. Reading his notebook out loud — word for word, pages he hasn't written yet.

He runs. Hotel staff say that room was empty for six months. Renovation pending.

So who was reading his future?
