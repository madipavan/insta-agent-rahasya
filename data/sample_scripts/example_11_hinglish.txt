Foreign thriller + desi brain = this book hits different.

Guy inherits a castle in Scotland. Sounds fancy. It's a dump. Also someone died there last week.

Police say accident. He says no — the bruises were wrong, the timing was wrong, and the maid keeps leaving marigold garlands on the victim's door like it's some ritual.

He's not superstitious. But he's also not stupid.
